### 1. Google Open-Sources AX Agentic Orchestration Runtime

Google has introduced AX, an open-source agentic orchestration runtime for declaring tasks with workspaces and gateway specifications, built on Agent Substrate for sandboxed execution. The project aims to support billions of autonomous agent workloads per cluster, though it remains under active development with possible breaking changes. This signals growing consolidation around secure, high-scale agent infrastructure for practitioners. [Source-github](https://github.com/google/ax)

### 2. OmniEdu Releases Open Foundation Models for K-12 Learning and Teaching

OmniEdu has launched an open family of educational foundation models for K-12 learning and teaching, trained with a corpus combining over 100 educational resources and general instruction data. The models target curriculum understanding, learner-difficulty diagnosis, problem solving, and instructional support. If validated, they could accelerate domain-specific open models in education while raising new evaluation and data-quality questions. [Source-huggingface](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.23088)

### 3. Realtime-Venus: Full-Duplex Audio-Visual Interaction with Async Delegation

Researchers present Realtime-Venus, a proactive full-duplex interaction system built from two separately trained 9B models: one for audio-visual dialogue and one for spoken dialogue. The design targets continuous perception and timely, visually grounded responses in digital and physical environments. This pushes multimodal assistants closer to real-time, embodied interaction, though latency and deployment cost remain practical constraints. [Source-huggingface](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.13814)
